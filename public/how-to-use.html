How to Use Easy Image Resizer for monday.com

Welcome! This guide will help you install, set up, and get the most out of the Easy Image Resizer app for monday.com.

Installation Instructions

  1. Go to the monday.com Apps Marketplace and search for Easy Image Resizer.
  2. Click Install and follow the prompts to add the app to your monday.com account.
  3. Make sure you have a board with at least two file columns (one for original images, one for resized images).
  4. Open any item on your board, click the + Add View button, and select Easy Image Resizer from the list.

First-Time Use

  1. Open a board item and switch to the Image Resizer view.
  2. Select your source column (where your original images are stored).
  3. Select your target column (where resized images will be saved).
  4. Choose your resize mode: Square (same width & height) or Custom (set width & height).
  5. For square mode, pick Crop to Square (choose crop position) or Pad to Square (choose padding color).
  6. Enter your desired dimensions and click Resize Image.
  7. The resized image will appear in your target column!

Usage Examples

  • Crop to Square: Easily crop non-square images into perfect squares. Choose the framing that fits your content—top, center, or bottom.
  • Pad to Square: Make your images web-ready with smart padding. Add white or transparent borders to create perfect square visuals.
  • Example: Original image padded to a perfect square with white borders.
